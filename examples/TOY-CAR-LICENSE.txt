This project's toy-car.stl is a modified geometry-only derivative of ToyCar.glb.
Changes: removed the decorative fabric display base; combined body and glass;
gave zero-thickness display panels finite walls using a 1.5-model-unit inward
solidify modifier; voxel-remeshed at 0.65 / 160 of the chosen longest span;
decimated to approximately 28,000 triangles; placed the longest axis along X
and height along Z; normalized the final longest span to 160 model units.
Materials and textures are omitted. The converted STL approximates the source
geometry for uniform-volume scattering; cavities retained by the remesher remain
empty during the app's odd-even volume sampling.
The conversion is reproducible using scripts/prepare-toy-car.py with Blender.

The derived example is also provided under CC0 1.0 Universal. No license for
the application's other code is implied by this example's public-domain status.
